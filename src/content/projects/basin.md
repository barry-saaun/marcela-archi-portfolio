Positioned at the northern edge of the existing Dairy Building in The Basin, this intervention proposes a rainwater collection and filtration station that responds to the floodplain as a landscape of water management, ecology, and public occupation. Through a lightweight steel structure composed of folded galvanised steel panels, steel grating, and an integrated filtration system, the project slows, collects, and filters rainwater while creating an atmospheric space of sound, light, and cooling. More than a utilitarian intervention, the station becomes a place to pause, rest, and observe the movement of water, simultaneously questioning the future potential of the Dairy Building and proposing its reactivation as a public space once again.
