Agora is a modular, community-oriented social housing project built on a 5x5m grid system; it supports flexible unit typologies around a central courtyard, fostering a shared spatial identity and user/centred design. Located in Hawthorn, the site has a significant slope towards the west and is surrounded by private residences, Unimelb campus to the north, and a large green space on the southwest. Its CLT structure built over a concrete slab allows for rapid, low-impact assembly, reducing the carbon footprint while creating a warm, tactile quality that responds to the local context.
