Situated within the layered environment of Abbotsford Convent, this performance centre is a place for gathering that enables reflection and renewal. The proposal engages with the site's historic, cultural, and ecological depth through an architecture that listens and gives back. Stacked timber structures form porous walls that filter rain, light, and sound, transforming weather into experience. As water moves through the architecture, it is collected, cleansed, and returned to the land and the Yarra River, creating a living threshold between the built and the natural. The proposal seeks reconciliation with Country through mindful materiality, human scale, and sensory engagement.
