Designed to be a sustainable, climate-responsive building that provides students with a comfortable, healthy, and safe environment. The building's structure prioritises a simple load path into the foundations, providing rigidity and stability to endure Melbourne's climatic conditions. The structural layout enables an open-plan interior with flexible spaces that remain visually connected to the exterior green areas, University Square.

Design decisions, including the arrangement of spaces, materiality, services, and amenities, were resolved to reduce the environmental impact of the building whilst enhancing energy efficiency, accessibility, acoustics, and lighting quality.
